# Boompact

Boompact is an online multiplayer arcade racing game. Players drive cars around an arena
while bombs are handed out at random. A car carrying a bomb passes it on by crashing into a
car without one. When the round timer runs out, whoever still holds a bomb loses.

## Core features

- **Spawning:** the server spawns the cars, with one spawn point per player.
- **Driving:** custom car physics, a camera that follows the car, engine sounds and sound
  effects.
- **Bomb distribution:** at the start of each match, half of the players get a bomb at
  random.
- **Bomb transfer:** a collision passes the bomb to the other car; a car that has just
  received a bomb has a cooldown before it can pass it on.
- **End of match:** a match timer, an end-of-match banner and a win/lose screen for each
  player.
